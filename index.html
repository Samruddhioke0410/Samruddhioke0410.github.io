Embedded Systems · Real-Time · Team of 2

AirDrum Duel

The ideaNo drum kit, no problem. Two players swing motion-sensing boards like drumsticks and battle beat for beat. The hard part is making an invisible drum feel instant on a tiny microcontroller.

What I built

  • Wrote bare-metal firmware in Embedded C and ARM assembly for the FRDM-KL46Z (Cortex-M0+), splitting the game into six concurrent stages from sensor read to scoring.
  • Turned raw IMU readings, sampled every 10 ms, into drum-hit gestures, and used earliest-deadline-first scheduling so time-critical hits never wait behind slower work.
  • Wired it together with hardware timers, interrupt handlers, and UART for low-latency input and output.
